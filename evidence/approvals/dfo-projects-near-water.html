Projects near water

Protecting fish and fish habitat, standards and codes of practice, project review, authorizations for working in or near water.

Most requested

  • Standards
  • Codes of practice
  • Pathways of Effects
  • Fisheries Act Registry
  • Aquatic species at risk map
  • Indigenous Habitat Participation Program

Amendments to the Authorizations Concerning Fish and Fish Habitat Protection Regulations will streamline application requirements for authorizations under the Fisheries Act. Changes will take effect on October 15, 2026. Further information will be published in the coming months.

Services and information

Request a review of your project

Request a review of your project to find out if it is likely to result in the death of fish, or the harmful alteration, disruption or destruction of fish habitat.

Apply for an authorization

An authorization is required if your project is likely to result in the death of fish, or the harmful alteration, disruption or destruction of fish habitat.

Report a problem

Contact us if you see someone polluting, poaching, harmfully impacting fish and fish habitat or illegally fishing.
